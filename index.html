FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can apply?+

Anyone of any nationality who lives in Rwanda, is aged 18 to 35, has completed high school, has intermediate English, and has at least 1,000 followers combined across all social media platforms.

Can I apply if I am not Rwandan?+

Yes. Any nationality can apply, as long as you live in Rwanda.

Do I need a big following?+

You need at least 1,000 followers in total, counting all your social media platforms together. Beyond that, what matters is the quality of your work and the engagement it creates.

Does my video need to be already posted?+

Yes. Your competition video must already exist and be posted on social media. It must be 3 minutes or longer and fit one of the three pillars. You share its link in the form, together with a pitch video of 1 minute or more.

Should I create content in English?+

No. English, Kinyarwanda, French and Swahili are all allowed. The most important thing is to create content that solves a real problem for real people. That will be proven by engagement and impact.

How are creators selected?+

We review the video link you shared, your pitch video of 1 minute or more, and your answers in the form, to see that you meet the criteria.

What happens after I am selected?+

To be among the top 3, you will create more content in collaboration with the other selected creators: interviewing them and being interviewed. Engagement counts, so remember all the scoring elements: your video, your pitch, your collaborations, and the engagement and impact they create.

Which platforms count?+

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or any major social media platform.

How do I apply?+

Click Apply Now, fill in the form, share your video link, upload your pitch video, and answer the questions before 9 October 2026.
